General Info

  • The report summarizes win loss data of Cash Out tickets based on selected currency.
  • This report contains only won/lose/draw tickets.
  • This report supports up-to-date data.

Search Filters

  • Report by: Data can be represented by Daily or Monthly.
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from test currency.
  • Currency: Selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
    • All: All supported currencies of will be listed out.
    • Based EUR/USD/GBP: All data will be converted to EUR/USD/GBP for reference only. The based currency list contains EUR, USD, GBP and dynamic options. The dynamic option will vary when a specific currency is selected in Currency field (except EUR, USD and GBP currency).

Report Guidelines

  • Date (mm/dd/yyyy): can be daily or monthly based on your selection.
  • Currency: your offered currency. To view in detail click on each currency.
  • Cash Out Times: number of times that members cash out bet tickets.
  • Cash Out Tickets: number of bet tickets that members cash out.
    Note that member can cash out a ticket several times.
  • Cash Out Value: the total stake amount of Cash Out tickets.
  • BuyBack Amount: the amount has been spent to buy Cash Out tickets.
  • Ticket Payout: the total amount you need to pay to House based on Cash Out tickets.
  • Cash Out Commission: the commission amount you received based on Cash Out tickets.
  • Amount Due: the settlement amount that you have paid or received based on Cash Out Product.
    Note that for Amount Due, the negative number indicates the receiving amount from House and the positive number indicates the paying amount to House.
