General Info

  • This function shows Total bets in each bet type of number game.

Search Filters

  • Currency: filters tickets placed in selected currency.
    • All: tickets of all supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Based EUR/USD: All figures will be converted to EUR/USD for reference only. The based currency list contains EUR, USD and a dynamic option. The dynamic option will vary when a specific currency is selected in Currency field (except EUR and USD currency).

Report Guidelines

  • Warrior: The customer may place bets at any time but before the 1st ball drawn
    • 2nd ball win: compare second ball drawn with first ball, whereby the ball drawn with highest number considered win.
      Total bets = total stakes on 2nd ball win
    • 3rd ball win: compare third ball drawn with first ball, whereby the ball drawn with highest number considered win.
      Total bets = total stakes on 3rd ball win
  • 1st ball/Last ball: first/last ball draw. The customer may place bets at any time but before the 1st ball drawn
    • Over/Under (O/U): predict the first/last ball is over or under a defined number. The positive black number means total turnover for Over greater than total turnover for Under, whereas the negative red number means total turnover for Under greater than total turnover for Over.
      Total bets = total stakes on Over – total stakes on Under
    • Odd/Even (O/E): predict whether the first/last ball is odd or even. The positive black number means total turnover for Odd greater than total turnover for Even, whereas the negative red number means total turnover for Even greater than total turnover for Odd.
      Total bets = total stakes on Odd – total stakes on Even
  • Next Ball: After each ball is drawn, the Company will offer odds on the next ball drawn. The customer may place bets at any time after the game starts. The game ends when a certain number of balls have been drawn, at the discretion of the Company.
    • Over/Under (O/U): predict whether the next ball will be over or under a defined number. The positive black number means total turnover for Over greater than total turnover for Under, whereas the negative red number means total turnover for Under greater than total turnover for Over.
      Total bets = total stakes on Over – total stakes on Under
    • Odd/Event (O/E): predict whether the next ball is odd or even. The positive black number means total turnover for Odd greater than total turnover for Even, whereas the negative red number means total turnover for Even greater than total turnover for Odd.
      Total bets = total stakes on Odd – total stakes on Even
    • High/Low: predict whether the next ball will have a higher or lower number. The positive black number means total turnover for High greater than total turnover for Low, whereas the negative red number means total turnover for Low greater than total turnover for High.
      Total bets = total stakes on High – total stakes on Low
  • Next Combo: After each ball is drawn, the Company will offer odds on whether the next ball drawn will be Over/Odd, Under/Odd, Over/Even and Under/Even. The customer may place bets at any time after the game starts. The game ends when a certain number of balls have been drawn, at the discretion of the Company.
    Total bets = total stakes on a bet type
  • First time Odd/Even (FT.O/E): predict sum of first three balls drawn is an odd or even number. The positive black number means total turnover for Odd greater than total turnover for Even, whereas the negative red number means total turnover for Even greater than total turnover for Odd.
    Total bets = total stakes on Odd – total stakes on Even
  • Number Wheel: bet on a group of numbers
    • Pre Game: customers may place bets before the game starts, but once the game starts no bets will allowed.
      Total bets = total stakes on Pre Games
    • Live Game: customers may place bets after the game starts and different odds will be given for every single ball drawn
      Total bets = total stakes on Live Game
