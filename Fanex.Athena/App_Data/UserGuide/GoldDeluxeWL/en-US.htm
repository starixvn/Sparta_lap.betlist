General Info

  • The report summarizes daily/monthly win loss and customer tip amount in Gold Deluxe.
  • Any query before 4PM GMT-4 will return the data up to 4PM of the day before.
  • You can filter data up to 3 months per query.

Search Filters

  • Currency: selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
  • Base Currency: convert all data to based currency for reference only.
  • Exclude Test Data: excludes all tickets from test account.

Report Guidelines

  • Date (mm/dd/yyyy): can be daily or monthly based on the selection.
  • Currency: offered currency has been selected by user. For more detail win loss, click on each currency.
  • Turnover: customer turnover within the selected date range.
  • Total Transaction: total bet tickets of selected date.
  • Customer Win/Loss: the amount customer won or lost based on their bets.
  • Licensee W/L: the win loss amount Licensee has settled with Customer. A red negative number indicates a loss for Licensee whereas the black positive indicates a win.
  • Tip Amount: total customer tip within the selected date range.
  • Tip Sharing: the amount that licensee received based on Tip amount.
  • Amount Due: the settlement amount that Licensee has paid to or received from company.

Note that: for Amount Due, the black negative number indicates the Licensee receiving amount and black positive number indicates the Licensee paying amount
