General Info

  • Data are available from 05/01/2012 up to the now.
  • This report contains only won/lose/draw tickets.
  • Data are represented by Daily.

Search Filters

  • Currency: selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
    • All: all supported currencies will be listed out.
    • Based EUR/ USD: All data will be converted to EUR/ USD for reference only.
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from test account.
  • Username: Enter the external ID or system ID of the searched account.
    Note that, if User has not specified any Customer account then the Report will list out data of all Customers within the selected date range.
  • Product: selects the Product type which data will be retrieved.
    Note that, there is no BA from product filters (BA win loss, turnover and related data already are included in Sportsbook).

Report Guidelines

  • Day: the list of days having data within the selected date range.
  • Opening Balance: this is Customer Opening Balance, the amount of funds in Customer account at the beginning of a day.
  • Deposit: The total amount Customer has deposited for each day.
  • Withdrawal: Customer withdrawal amount.
  • Bonus: the bonus amount that Licensee has given to Customer.
  • Adjustment: the total adjustment amount Licensee used to adjust the Customer balance.
  • Turnover: Customer turnover for each day within the selected date range.
  • Draw Game: the total stake of draw tickets.
  • Net Turnover: the amount of turnover after deducting draw tickets.
    • Net Turnover = Turnover - Draw game.
  • Pending Funds: total stake of running tickets.
  • Customer W/L: customer net win loss for each day.
  • Closing Balance: this is Customer Closing Balance, the amount of funds at the end of a day.
    • Closing Balance = Opening Balance + Deposit – Withdrawal + Bonus + Adjustment – Pending funds + Customer W/L
  • Affiliate Transfer: the total payout for affiliate.

BA

  • Turnover: total Customer stake of BA tickets
    Note that, BA Turnover is for reference only, because it is included in the overall Turnover.
  • Comm: Licensee commission income for BA tickets
  • Amount Due To: the settlement amounts between Licensee and BA service.
    Note that for Amount Due to BA, the black negative number indicates the receiving amount from BA and black positive number indicates the paying amount to BA
