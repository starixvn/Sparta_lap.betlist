General Info

  • This function provides forecasts for matches that have Odd/Even, 1X2, and Draw No Draw (DND) bets.
  • Odd/Even means betting that is determined by whether the total number of points (goals, games etc) in the final result of an event is Odd or Even.
  • 1st Half Odd/Even means betting that is determined by whether the total number of points at the end of the first half of an event is Odd or Even.
  • 1X2 means betting in any one of three possible winning outcomes for an Event. 1 refers to the team that is named first (usually the home team); X refers to the game resulting in a draw or tie; 2 refers to the team that is named second (usually the away team).
  • Draw No Draw: customers will have two options to bet is either Draw or No Draw (no matter home or away who will win).

Search Filters

  • Currency: filters tickets placed in selected currency.
    • All: tickets of all supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Based EUR/USD: All figures will be converted to EUR/USD for reference only. The based currency list contains EUR, USD and a dynamic option. The dynamic option will vary when a specific currency is selected in Currency field (except EUR and USD currency).

Report Guidelines

  • Odd/Even: indicates there are more bets on either Odd or Even.
    Total Bets Odd/Even = Total Odd - Total Even
    • A positive number (e.g. 10) indicates more turnover on 'Odd'.
    • A negative number (e.g. –10) indicates more turnover on 'Even'.
  • 1X2: betting in any one of three possible winning outcomes for an event:
    Total Bets 1X2 = Stake of each bet ticket
    • 1 refers to the team that is named first (usually home team).
      Forecast Home = (home stake * odds) – (draw stake + away stake)
    • X refers to the game resulting in a draw or tie.
      Forecast Draw = (draw stake * odds) – (home stake + away stake)
    • 2 refers to the team that is named second (usually away team).
      Forecast Away = (away stake * odds) – (draw stake + home stake)
