General Info

  • The report summarizes daily/monthly win loss in Playtech Casino.

Search Filters

  • Currency: selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
    • All: all supported currencies will be listed out.
    • Based EUR/USD: All data will be converted to EUR/USD for reference only. The based currency list contains EUR, USD and a dynamic option. The dynamic option will vary when a specific currency is selected in Currency field (except EUR and USD currency).
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from test account.
  • Report by: Data can be represented by Daily or Monthly.

Report Guidelines

  • Currency: offered currency has been selected by user.
  • Turnover: Customer turnover within the selected date range.
  • Total Transaction: total bet tickets of selected products.
  • Customer W/L:
    • Win Loss: the amount customer won or lost based on their bets.
    • Comm: total amount of bonus counted on bet tickets.
    • Total: Total win/loss amount including commission.
      Total = Win/Loss + Commission
  • Licensee W/L: the win loss amount Licensee has settled with Customer.
    Licensee Win/Loss = – (Customer Win/Loss)
  • P.Share: a share amount that member must contribute to a prize pool.
  • P.Win: a winning amount that member can win from a prize pool.
  • Net Gaming: net revenue of company from Playtech Casino after excluding P.Share.
