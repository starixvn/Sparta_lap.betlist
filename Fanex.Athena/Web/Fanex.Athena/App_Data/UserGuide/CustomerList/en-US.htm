General Info

  • This function shows all customer information along with their settings.

Search Filters

  • Username: searched by External ID or System ID.
  • Currency: select the currency of customers that you want to manage.
    • All: all supported currencies will be listed out.
    • Based EUR/USD: All figures will be converted to EUR/USD for reference only. The based currency list contains EUR, USD and a dynamic option. The dynamic option will vary when a specific currency is selected in Currency field (except EUR and USD currency).
  • Special Level: view customers of a specific special level.
  • Group: view customers of a specific group.
  • Show/hide: show or hide columns of the report.

Report Guidelines

  • Move to group: move customer(s) to another group.
  • Update Commission: change commission value or customer(s).
  • Remove from group: remove customer(s) from their group commission.
    • Keep current commission: remove customer from his group, but still keep his current commission rates.
    • Clear customer’s commission: remove customer from his group and clear all his commission rates.
  • Available Funds: indicate available amount to place bets.
  • Pending Fund: indicate total stakes of all running bets.
  • Special Level: view or set special level for customer.
  • Customer Option: number of viewing options including:
    • Bet Setting: view bet setting for Sportsbook, Number Game, Virtual Sports, Live Casino and RNG Casino products.
    • Bet List: view list of bet tickets based on Euro during a selected date range.
    • Statement: view daily statements of customer up to 4 months. All transactions are summarized and then put into daily record. Each daily record balance will be sum up to the current date as latest balance.
    • Log Info: view login information of customer.
