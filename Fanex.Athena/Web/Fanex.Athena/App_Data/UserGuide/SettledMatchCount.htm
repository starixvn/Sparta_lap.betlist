General Info
  • The report lists out total number of events for yesterday and today. Note that RNG Casino and Bingo are not included in the report.
  • Term definition
  • Result: the list of all sport types having events for yesterday and today.
  • Entered: shows the total numbers of events have been settled and results have been entered into the system for each sport types.
  • Non-Entered: shows the total numbers of events have not been settled and results result have not been keyed into the system.
