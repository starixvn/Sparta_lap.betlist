General Info

  • The report summarizes daily/monthly win loss in Colossus Bets.
  • Data can be represented by Daily or Monthly.

Search Filters

  • Currency: selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
  • All: all supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Based EUR/USD/GBP: All data will be converted to EUR/USD/GBP for reference only. The based currency list contains EUR, USD, GBP and dynamic options. The dynamic option will vary when a specific currency is selected in Currency field (except EUR, USD and GBP currency).
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from test account.

Report Guidelines

  • Currency: offered currency has been selected by user.
  • Turnover: Customer turnover within the selected date range.
  • Total Transaction: total bet tickets of selected date.
  • Amt <30k GBP: added into customer balance.
  • Member's Winnings: the winning amount of Jackpot/Consolation prizes.
  • Cash-out Amt: the selling amount of the ticket or a part of the ticket.
  • Cash-out Share: licensee receiving amount when customers sell the tickets.
  • Commission Share: the amount that licensee received based on turnover of Colossus Bet product.
  • Ammount Due To Colossus Bets: the settled amount with Colossus Bets.
    • Positive: paid to Colossus Bets.
    • Negative: received from Colossus Bets.
  • Amt >30k GBP: directly paid by Licensee.
