General Info

  • The report contains only won/lose/draw tickets.
  • Data are available since 01/01/2010 up to now.
  • A red negative number indicates a loss for Licensee whereas the black positive indicates a win.

Search Filters

  • Report by: Data can be represented by Daily or Monthly.
  • Draw filter:
    • Normal: All won, lose and draw tickets will be included.
    • No Draws: Only won and lose tickets will be included.
    • No draws For Turnover Only: Draw tickets will not be counted in Turnover value.
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from test account.
  • Product: select the Product type of bet tickets.
    • If BA is not selected: BA commission and Amount due to BA columns will be hidden.
    • If Racing is not selected: Racing Revenue and Amount Due to Racing will be hidden.
  • Currency: selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
  • Currency: selects offered currencies of the bet tickets that will be listed out in the report.
  • Based: specifies the based currency which data will be displayed. The based currency list contains EUR, USD and a dynamic option. The dynamic option will vary when a specific currency is selected in Currency field (except EUR and USD currency).
  • Stake range: filter tickets that have stakes placed in selected range.
    • All: all supported currencies of will be listed out.
    • Based EUR/ USD: All data will be converted to EUR/ USD for reference only.

Report Guidelines

  • Date: can be daily or monthly based on user selection and its format is (mm/dd/yyyy).
  • Currency: offered currency has been selected by User. To view in detail click on each currency.
  • Turnover: total Customer turnover of all selected products within a selected date range.
    Note that for Turnover, the accumulated data of selected products will be displayed first. To see data separated by products, users can click on the Expand icon.
  • Total Transaction: total bet tickets of selected products.
  • Customer W/L: customer net winloss within a selected date range.
  • Licensee W/L: the winloss amount Licensee has settled with Customer.
    Licensee Win/Loss = – (Customer Win/Loss)
    Note that for Licensee W/L, the accumulated data of selected products will be displayed first. To see data separated by products, users can click on the Expand icon.
  • Racing Revenue: Licensee Commission received from Racing Partner.
  • Amount Due To BA:the settlement amounts between Licensee and BA service Note that for Amount Due to BA, the black negative number indicates the receiving amount from BA and black positive number indicates the paying amount to BA.
  • Amount Due to Racing:the settlement amount between Licensee and Racing partner Note that for Amount Due to Racing, the black negative number indicates the receiving amount from Racing partner and black positive number indicates the paying amount to Racing Partner.
