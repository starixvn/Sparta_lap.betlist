General Info

  • This report shows the total win/loss amount for the specified period sorted by different bet types.
  • This report contains only won/lose/draw tickets.
  • Data can be retrieved from maximum last 14 days up to the current date.
  • A red negative number indicates a loss for Licensee whereas the black positive indicates a win.

Search Filters

  • Currency: selects the currency which data will be retrieved
    • All: data of all supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Based EUR/USD: All data will be converted to EUR/ USD for reference only. The based currency list contains EUR, USD and a dynamic option. The dynamic option will vary when a specific currency is selected in Currency field (except EUR and USD currency).

Report Guidelines

  • Game Type Name: lists out all supported bettypes.
  • Turnover: total Customer turnover within a selected date range.
  • Customer W/L: customer net win loss within a selected date range.
  • Licensee W/L: the win loss amount Licensee has settled with Customer.
    Licensee Win/Loss = – (Customer Win/Loss)
  • BA Commission: Licensee commission income for BA tickets (including win, lose and draw tickets)
  • Racing Revenue: Licensee Commission received from Racing Partner.
  • Amount Due To BA: the settlement amounts between Licensee and BA service.
    Note that for Amount Due to BA, the black negative number indicates the receiving amount from BA and black positive number indicates the paying amount to BA
  • Amount Due to Racing: Licensee Commission received from Racing Partner.
    Note that for Amount Due to Racing, the black negative number indicates the receiving amount from Racing partner and black positive number indicates the paying amount to Racing Partner.
